CHED MSRS Scholarship Benefits & Financial Package

Republic Act No. 11509 (Doktor Para sa Bayan Act)

Establishes the Medical Scholarship and Return Service (MSRS) Program for deserving medical students in State Universities and Colleges (SUCs) and partner Private HEIs, with mandatory return service.

Scholarship Inclusions & Financial Allowances

Tuition & School Fees

100% covered for SUCs. Up to ₱100,000–₱105,000 per semester for partner Private HEIs (with institutional counterpart balance coverage).

Living Stipend

₱30,000 per semester (₱60,000 annually) direct monthly subsistence support.

Lodging & Housing Allowance

₱30,000 per semester accommodation allowance.

Book & Equipment Allowance

₱13,000 per year for textbooks, medical supplies, and equipment.

Transportation & Connectivity

₱6,000/sem travel allowance plus ₱6,000/yr connectivity & miscellaneous fees.

Internship & Board Review

Full coverage for Post-Graduate Internship (PGI) stipend and Physician Licensure Exam (PLE) review fees.

Return Service Requirement (RSA): Scholars serve 1 year in a government health facility or priority community for every 1 year of scholarship received.
